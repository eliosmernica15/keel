How it works

From first thought to finished work

Ask for what you need, shape it together, and keep every version close to the conversation that made it.

  1. 01

    Edit your work

    Ask Keel to draft a document or slide, then edit the result directly. Every change stays yours.

  2. 02

    Work with your team

    Bring people into the same artifact, leave comments in context, and move forward with one shared copy.

  3. 03

    Figure out

    Turn a rough question into a clear answer, with the notes, sources, and next steps attached.

  4. 04

    Figure out with your data

    Connect your numbers and ask the question behind them. Keel builds the view and explains the work.
