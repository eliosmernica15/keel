Privacy — what Keel does not collect

Keel is a local-first AI workspace. Your conversations, files and credentials stay on your machine unless you explicitly choose to send them to a provider or channel.

Keel does not

  • send telemetry, analytics or crash reports to Keel;
  • require an account, cloud service or subscription;
  • read your files except through tools you approve;
  • store your provider keys anywhere except your own local settings;
  • record audio or screen content — dictation runs in your browser.

What leaves your machine

When you use a remote model provider, the prompt and tool schemas are sent to that provider. Keel redacts credential-shaped content before sending, and records an egress log locally so you can see what left.

Optional integrations (webhooks, email, calendars, channels) send data only when you configure them and only to the destination you specify.

Your controls

  • Read-only sessions, dry-run mode and an egress allowlist;
  • global kill switch to stop all agents and revoke client tokens;
  • recoverable trash, undo and an immutable audit trail;
  • local diagnostics bundles you choose whether to share.
