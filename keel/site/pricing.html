FAQ

The Pro plan gives you everything in a Free plan with more usage and more of KEEL's capabilities. That includes KEEL Code, along with projects to organize your chats and documents, access to more KEEL models, and KEEL for Microsoft 365. You can choose a monthly or annual subscription.

Max and Ultra are built for people who work with KEEL throughout the day and want longer, uninterrupted sessions. You get everything in Pro, plus more usage. Max gives you 5x the usage of Pro, every 5 hours and every week. Ultra gives you 10x the weekly usage of Pro, with no 5-hour limit for verified accounts. Both plans include Fable, our top model, for up to half your weekly limit, and a monthly API credit to build on the KEEL Platform: $100 on Max, $200 on Ultra. Both plans are billed monthly.

Every plan has usage limits. Most accounts have a limit that resets on a rolling five-hour session window, and paid plans add weekly limits on top. Your activity across KEEL on web, desktop, mobile, and KEEL Code all draws from the same pool of usage. How quickly you reach a limit depends on the model you use, the length of your conversations, and the size of the files you work with. When you reach a limit, you can switch to a less resource-intensive model and keep working until your window resets, or wait for the next window to begin. Free includes limited usage every five hours and every week, Pro includes 1x usage, Max includes 5x usage, and Ultra includes 10x weekly usage with no five-hour limit for verified accounts.

KEEL Code is included in all paid plans. It shares the same usage limits as the rest of your plan, so work you do in KEEL Code counts toward the same five-hour and weekly limits as conversations in KEEL on the web, on desktop, and on mobile. You can use KEEL Code in your terminal, in your editor, or from the KEEL desktop app, and your projects and settings follow you between them.

KEEL Enterprise is $20 per seat per month plus usage billed at API rates. The final price depends on the size of your organization, the features you need, and how your team uses KEEL, so contact our sales team for a quote. Enterprise includes central billing and administration, single sign-on, SCIM, audit logs, usage analytics, and custom data retention controls.

You are billed on the date you start your subscription, and your subscription renews on that same date each month or each year, depending on your billing cycle. If you switch plans partway through a cycle, the change is prorated so you only pay for the time you use the new plan.

Annual subscriptions are billed once a year and save you up to 20% compared with paying month to month. For example, Pro is $17 per month with an annual subscription instead of $20 when billed monthly. You can switch between monthly and annual billing at any time from your account settings.

Individual plans can be paid with a major credit card. Team and Enterprise customers can also arrange ACH transfers and invoicing with net terms. If you need a different payment arrangement, contact our sales team and we will help you find the right option.

Prices shown do not include applicable tax. Sales tax, value added tax, and other local taxes are calculated at checkout based on your billing address and are added to your invoice.

If you are not satisfied with your subscription, contact us within 14 days of your purchase and we will review your request for a refund. Refunds are handled on a case-by-case basis, and usage already consumed may be taken into account.

Open your account settings, choose your plan, and select Cancel subscription. Your paid features remain available until the end of the current billing period, after which your account returns to the Free plan. Your conversations and projects stay in your account.

Usage limits and monthly API credits reset to the levels of your new plan as soon as an upgrade takes effect. Credits from your previous plan do not carry over, and unused credits are not paid out in cash. Your existing conversations and projects are not affected.
