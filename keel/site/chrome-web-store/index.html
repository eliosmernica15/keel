About Keel in Chrome

Keel is a browser companion that works in the tabs you already have open. Ask it to read a page, fill in a form or click through a workflow — it does the work while you stay in charge of every action.

  • ✦Reads the page. Keel understands the document, table or article in front of you and answers questions about it without leaving the tab.
  • ▸Clicks, types and fills forms. Hand off repetitive steps — Keel navigates, enters data and completes multi-step tasks across sites.
  • ◇You approve what happens. Nothing is submitted, sent or changed until you review and approve the action.
  • ⇄Works across tabs. Keep context as you move between pages, and continue a task wherever you left off.

Install the unpacked extension in a few steps. It's local-first — no account required.

  1. 1
    Download the extension zip from the link below.
  2. 2
    Unzip the archive into a folder you can find again.
  3. 3
    Open chrome://extensions and enable Developer mode (top right).
  4. 4
    Choose Load unpacked and select the unzipped folder.
