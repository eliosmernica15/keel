Capabilities

An agent with real hands.

Keel is not a chat box with delusions of agency. It has a working agent loop, a shell parser that knows what commands actually do, and a permission model that stands between the model and your machine.

Coding

Reads, patches and writes against your real workspace. The agent loop runs against a folder you bind — not a sandboxed re-creation of it.

runner.py · shell_ast.py

Terminal

Shell commands are parsed into an AST before execution. Allow-lists, deny-lists, and permission gates — the model never gets a raw shell by default.

shell_ast.py · permissions.py

Files & workspace

Bind a folder as the agent's workspace. Files it asks you to attach are uploaded to ~/.keel/uploads and read by the agent itself.

~/.keel/uploads · workspace binding

Sessions & fleet

Named sessions with transcripts, a multi-agent fleet with delegation and groups, routines and scheduling — persistent across restarts.

session.py · fleet/ · transcript.py

Tools & workflows

21 bundled skills, user skills loaded from disk, hooks, subagents, slash commands. Automate the workflow you already have.

skills.py · hooks.py · subagent.py · commands.py

MCP & extensibility

A Model Context Protocol client for tool servers, plus plugins, skills and an OpenCode-shaped REST/events surface for client tooling.

mcp.py · /api/v2 (tracer) · plugins

Providers

Your keys.
Your models.
Auto failover.

Bring your own keys. Keel verifies each key against the provider before it saves it, then sends it nowhere else. Flip providers, pin a model, or let the auto-chain fall through in order.

  • OpenRouterwired · browser & bridge
  • Google AI Studiowired · free tier available
  • Anthropic APIopt-in only · your terms
  • OpenAI-compatible endpointsany base URL · bridge

Auto chain: openrouter → google → nvidia → openai-compatible, resolved per request from your saved order. The web mirror wires OpenRouter and Google AI Studio; the desktop build adds Anthropic (opt-in) and OpenAI-compatible endpoints.

Local-first

Your machine is
the trust boundary.

  • Runs where you run. Bridge on 127.0.0.1 with a per-launch token in the URL — never a relay we operate, never reachable from the network.
  • No telemetry. There is no analytics, no crash reporter, no usage beacon anywhere in the codebase — this is auditable in the repository.
  • Manual permission mode. Privileged tool calls wait for your explicit approve/reject — visibly, in the UI you just saw.
  • ~/.keel is yours. Sessions, settings, keys and uploads live on your disk. Uninstalling Keel Desktop never deletes them.
  • No account, no billing inside the app. The device is the account. Your provider bill is your provider's business.
