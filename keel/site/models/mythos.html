MYTHOS Mythos · multimodal core

What it is designed for

A concept with a clear point of view

Four things we would want Mythos to be genuinely good at, if we ever built it.

01

Cross-format reasoning

Hold a contract, a whiteboard photo, a call recording and a spreadsheet in the same thought, then reason across all four.

02

Long, patient inquiry

Stay with an ambiguous question through dozens of turns without losing the thread or the evidence behind it.

03

Structured judgment

Turn messy inputs into a decision you can defend, with the assumptions and the trade-offs made explicit.

04

Room for a creative side

Fable would share the reasoning engine but loosen the style for stories, worlds and play.

Illustrative capability grid

Concept targets, not specifications

Ideal tasks

Creative variant · concept

Fable — the creative side of Mythos

Fable is an imagined variant of Mythos rather than a separate model. It would keep the same reasoning core but trade strict neutrality for voice, story and play. Like everything on this page, it is a concept only.

WorldbuildingKeep a setting consistent across characters, history and rules.
Narrative voiceTry a scene in several voices without losing the plot.
Visual storytellingWork from images and text together to shape a sequence.
