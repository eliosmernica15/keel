Keel Duet is a proactive agent you can talk to in Keel. Tell it what needs to happen and it works on its own computer in the cloud, using the apps and tools you connect. It keeps going between conversations, checks in when it needs your decision, and hands the finished work back to you.

Start a chat and describe the outcome you want. Keel Duet makes a plan, connects to the tools you've approved, and works through each step. When it reaches something that needs your judgment, it stops and asks — then picks up right where it left off once you reply.

Keel Code and Cowork are thinking partners that work with you in the moment. Keel Duet is that same intelligence with time and initiative of its own: it can work across apps, keep track of open threads, and make progress while you're away, always within the boundaries you set.

It can triage an inbox, prepare a project brief, follow up on outstanding requests, organize notes and documents, research a topic, and move work between the tools your team already uses. You can hand off a single task or an entire project.

We're opening Keel Duet gradually through the waitlist. Joining costs nothing; we'll share details about plans and pricing before anything changes, and you can decide then whether it's right for you.

Each task runs on its own cloud computer with the connectors you approve. Long-running work keeps going between conversations, and you'll see an activity feed so you can follow every step and step in whenever a decision is yours.

You can start in the Keel app on the web and desktop, and it works across the apps and tools you connect. Because it runs in the cloud, it keeps working even when you close your laptop.

We're shaping the rollout with the people on the waitlist. Next up are more connectors, richer approval controls, and support for longer projects across your browser and mobile device.
