FAQ

Keel Duet is a helpful, proactive agent you chat with on the Keel app. Tell it what you need to work on and it works on its own computer in the cloud, using apps and tools you connect. It keeps going between conversations, checks in when it needs your decision, and hands the finished work back to you.

Start a chat and describe the outcome you want. Keel Duet makes a plan, connects to the tools you've approved, and works through each step. When it reaches something that needs your judgment, it stops and asks — then picks up right where it left off once you reply.

Keel is your thinking partner in the conversation. Keel Duet is that same intelligence with time and initiative of its own: it can work across apps, keep track of open threads, and make progress while you're away, always within the boundaries you set.

It can triage an inbox, prepare a project brief, follow up on outstanding requests, organize notes and documents, research a topic, and move work between the tools your team already uses. You can hand off a single task or an entire project.

Keel Duet is included in the waitlist at no cost when it becomes available. We'll share details about plans and pricing before anything changes, and you can decide then whether it's right for you.

We're rolling it out gradually. Join the waitlist and we'll let you know as soon as a spot opens up for you and your team.

You can start in the Keel app on the web and desktop, and it works across the apps and tools you connect. Because it runs in the cloud, it keeps working even when you close your laptop.

Just tell it to stop, or use the pause control in the activity feed. Any action that needs your approval is already waiting on you, so nothing moves forward until you say so.

Yes. Keel Duet follows the same privacy and security standards as the rest of Keel. It only uses the apps and data you connect, it asks before acting on anything important, and you can review or revoke access at any time.
