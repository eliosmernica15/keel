Frequently asked questions

Keel Science is a research partner that walks with you through the research process, from shaping a question to writing it up. It reads the literature, works with your files and figures, and keeps your sources attached to the work.

It is for anyone doing rigorous research: students, doctoral candidates, postdocs, lab teams, and independent investigators. It is available to everyone, not just one institution or field.

It is built around research workflows rather than one-off answers: sources stay linked, papers and code can be opened in place, and the work continues across the whole project from idea to publication.
