How it works

Tag Keel in any Slack thread to bring Keel into the conversation. For a task that is part of an ongoing thread, it knows what's going on and can jump back in.

  1. 01

    Tag Keel

    Mention @Keel in a channel or thread and ask your question the way you would ask a teammate.

  2. 02

    For a task that is part of an ongoing thread

    Keel reads the messages above your mention, so it understands the decision, the people involved and what has already been tried.

  3. 03

    It knows what's going on and can jump back in

    When the thread continues, Keel follows along. It picks up new context and can pick the work back up when you tag it again.

Core capabilities

See what Keel can do for your team.

Keel reads new messages, links and attachments in the channels you allow, and turns them into structured requests without anyone copying notes into another tool.

When a question turns into a thread, Keel collects the relevant messages and proposes a next step with an owner and a status, so nothing gets lost in the scroll.

Keel asks focused clarifying questions one at a time, using what the team has already said, and updates its answer as new information arrives in the thread.

Keel can pull in linked files, dashboards and past decisions from the tools you connect, then cite them in its reply so teammates can check the source.

Using channel rules and your directory, Keel assigns work to the person or team best placed to handle it, and notifies them in the same thread.

Keel can gather the evidence, draft the response and complete the workflow, then post the outcome back in Slack so the whole team sees the resolution.

Every resolved request becomes a searchable entry with its sources and decision history, so the next person can find the answer without asking again.

Admins control which channels Keel can read, who can tag it and what it is allowed to do, with an audit log of every action. See admin controls.
