FAQ

Keel Security is in public beta for Keel Enterprise. Contact sales to ask about access for your team.

Access is scoped to the repositories you choose to connect and the permissions you grant. Keel Security suggests fixes for your review; nothing changes in your codebase until you approve it.

AI-assisted review can miss an issue or propose a change that needs a second look. Keep a human in the loop, review every suggested fix, and grant only the access you need.

Keel reasons about data flows in context, for example an untrusted login value reaching password recovery. It surfaces issues that matter and filters out likely false positives.

Instead of matching patterns alone, Keel Mythos reads your code like a researcher, validates risks in context, and suggests and tracks fixes with your approval.

It is designed to complement the scanners and workflows your team already uses: review Keel findings alongside your existing reports. Contact sales to discuss your setup.
