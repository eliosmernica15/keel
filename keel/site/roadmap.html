FAQ

Is Keel free?

Yes. No account or paid service is required; optional integrations use your own credentials.

Where does my data go?

It stays local unless you send a prompt to a provider or configure a channel. See Privacy.

Can I run fully offline?

Yes — use the built-in deterministic fake and local tools.
