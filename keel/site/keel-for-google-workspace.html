Frequently asked questions

The essentials before your team tries the Beta.

It is a Beta extension that brings Keel into Google Docs, Sheets, and Slides. Open a file you already have, ask Keel for help in the side panel, and review every change before it lands.

The Microsoft 365 app works in Word, Excel, and PowerPoint. This extension is built for Google Docs, Sheets, and Slides. Both use the same Keel account and the same accept-or-edit review step.

Ask your Google Workspace admin to enable the Keel add-on for your domain. Then open a Doc, Sheet, or Slide and look for Keel under the Extensions menu. If it is not there yet, your admin has not enabled it.

In the Keel side panel of a supported Google file, or in the Keel desktop app. Your conversations stay with your Keel account, so you can pick up where you left off at /login.

Keel can only read a file a user explicitly shares. Workspace admins control whether the extension is available, and every edit needs your approval. Keel does not read the rest of Drive.

Email hello@keel.ai with “Google Workspace Beta” in the subject, or ask your admin to get in touch. Our policies are listed in Privacy and Terms.

Try Keel for Google Workspace

The Beta add-on is enabled by your Workspace admin. These are the real next steps.

  1. Sign in to Keel — use the same account across desktop and web.
  2. Install the desktop app if you want the same workflows outside Google files.
  3. Ask your Google Workspace admin to enable the Keel add-on for your domain, then open a Doc, Sheet, or Slide and choose Extensions → Keel.

The Google Workspace Marketplace listing is not part of this repository yet. If Keel is missing from the Extensions menu, your admin has not enabled it. Email hello@keel.ai for setup help.
