Core capabilities

Reach the web that has no API

Access internal tools, legacy portals, admin consoles, and invoice systems. Keel works with the logins you have, on the tab in front of you. Drag more tabs into Keel's tab group and Keel works across all of them.

Your whole setup, in the side panel

The skills, plugins, and connectors you've already built are available in your side panel session, with nothing to set up in the browser.

Your session follows you

Every conversation saves to your history. Sessions live with your account, not the machine, so you can start in Chrome and pick it up on desktop or mobile without needing to re-explain. A skill you save in the panel runs from any surface too.

Three ways to access the extension

Access Keel in Chrome through the side panel when the work is on the page in front of you, and nothing else needs to be open. You can also access it in Keel when the browser is just one step in a longer job, or in Keel Code when you're building the site you're testing.

FAQ

Keel in Chrome is a browser extension that lets Keel work on the web pages you have open. Keel can read a page, then click, type, or fill forms on the sites you're already signed in to. You choose whether Keel asks before it acts or works through a task with a safety check before each action. Common use cases include testing web pages during development, gathering research from across the web, and helping with repetitive tasks like data entry. Open it from the Chrome side panel, from Keel, or from Keel Code.

Keel uses a browser two ways. If you already use Keel in Chrome, that stays your default and Keel keeps working in your Chrome browser, where you're already signed in. Switch anytime in Settings → Capabilities → Preferred browser. Keel also includes its own built-in browser, so it can spin up work on its own without taking over your screen, and your work stays safe. There's nothing to install. If you're new to browser use, Keel uses the built-in browser by default.

Avoid financial transactions, password management, or anything involving sensitive personal data. Start with trusted sites and familiar workflows where you're comfortable having Keel take actions. Make sure your use complies with our acceptable use policy, and learn more in the safety guide.

Browser AI faces unique security risks, like prompt injection attacks, where malicious actors might try to trick Keel into unintended actions, such as sharing your bank information or deleting important files. While we've implemented protections, they aren't foolproof. Attack vectors are constantly evolving and Keel may hallucinate, leading to actions that you did not intend. We've shared our testing results, including possible attack scenarios, so you can make informed decisions, and strongly encourage you to read about the risks before using this product.

Keel Cowork is now just Keel, so you don't have to choose where to get work done. The update is rolling out to Pro and Max plans, with more to follow. If you're on a different plan, you'll be walked through the update in the product. We hope you enjoy the simpler experience.
