FAQ

Open any HTTPS page in Chrome and click the Keel toolbar icon. You can also right-click the page and choose “Ask Keel about this page”. The side panel reads page text read-only and keeps the answer next to the tab you are on.

Type a question in the composer at the bottom of the panel. Use the Chat or Cowork toggle to choose the kind of help you want, then review the result before copying it anywhere or approving an action.

Cowork is the workspace for jobs that span several steps and surfaces. The desktop app keeps that same work on your computer. Keel in Chrome brings the panel to the browser tab in front of you; sessions remain in your history.

The browser extension is for Chrome on desktop. On a phone, use the Keel web app in your mobile browser and sign in with the same account.

Yes. The panel follows the active tab, and you can group related tabs together so a task stays in context. Conversations are saved to your history so you can pick them up later.
