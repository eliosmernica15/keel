Frequently asked questions

Keel is a thinking partner that works where you do — on the web, on your desktop and on your phone. Ask a question, hand off a task, search the web, create files or run code, and Keel keeps the thread across conversations. You can connect the apps and tools you already use, and pick up a project on another device without starting over.

Use Keel to work through difficult questions, draft and refine documents, explore data, build prototypes with Keel Code, run research with Keel Science, and schedule repeatable tasks. It is built for everyday work and for long, ambitious projects that need more context and more room to think.

You can start for free on the Free plan. Pro is $17 per month with an annual subscription discount, Max and Ultra start from $100 per month. Teams are $25 per seat, and Enterprise pricing is custom. Usage limits apply, and no credit card is required to get started.
