Creating a skill

A skill is a reusable set of instructions that teaches Keel how your team handles a specific kind of work.

Keep the description short and concrete: say what the skill is for, when to use it, and what a good result looks like.

Writing a skill

Three things to get right before you share a skill with your team.

Say what the skill does and when Keel should use it. A clear trigger is more useful than a long list of instructions.

Include the steps your team actually repeats and the checks that define a good result. Leave room for judgement on everything else.

Keep personal skills in your own library and share team skills from a single company library. Both work in the desktop app and on the web.
